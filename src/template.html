How zones were picked

No hiding: crime in Scotland's worst 10% of data zones and overall deprivation in the worst 20%.

Public spots only: crime in the worst 20% and deprivation in the worst 30%, or crime in the worst 10% outside the centre.

Busy: worst-10% crime in the centre, business parks and the airport. Rates are per resident, so visitors inflate them. That's mostly theft, so these stay fully playable.

Crime figures are 2017–18 recorded crime (violence, sexual offences, housebreaking, vandalism, drugs, common assault). Treat old patterns as a guide, not a guarantee.
